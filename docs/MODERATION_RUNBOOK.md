# WorkTrade moderation runbook

## Purpose

Protect participants while applying the least restrictive proportionate action. WorkTrade is not an emergency service. Direct immediate threats to local emergency services and preserve relevant platform records.

## Access

- Reviewers may triage and document cases.
- Moderators may warn, restrict, suspend, resolve cases, and decide appeals.
- Administrators may also ban accounts and manage staff access.
- Staff access is personal. Never share sign-in links or sessions.

## Triage

1. Confirm the report target and preserve the relevant request, message, circle, or chain identifiers.
2. Mark credible immediate safety threats, violence, exploitation, or serious privacy exposure urgent.
3. Check for duplicate reports, active agreements, prior restrictions, and affected counterparties.
4. Keep allegations in internal notes. Reporter updates must contain only information safe to disclose.

## Actions

- Dismiss when the report is unsupported, mistaken, or outside policy.
- Warn for a correctable first incident with limited foreseeable harm.
- Restrict interactions while facts or required safety controls are verified.
- Suspend for serious or repeated misconduct where a time-limited removal is proportionate.
- Ban only for severe abuse, fraud, dangerous prohibited activity, evasion, or repeated serious violations.

Every action requires a factual internal rationale. Never put secrets, unnecessary personal data, medical information, or speculation in case notes.

## Appeals

1. Review the original evidence, decision, and the member's new information.
2. Use a different moderator when staffing permits.
3. Grant the appeal when the original basis is disproven, controls adequately reduce the risk, or the action was disproportionate.
4. Give the member a concise explanation. The immutable audit history records reinstatement.

## Operational checks

- Review the open queue at least once each operating day during a pilot.
- Address urgent cases first and notify affected active-work participants when necessary.
- Do not delete audit entries or privately promise a particular outcome.
- Escalate legal requests, credible threats, child-safety concerns, and suspected financial crime to qualified counsel or the appropriate authority.
