# Production decisions

These choices affect real people or are difficult to reverse. They should be made before activating the corresponding capability.

## Required to activate accounts

- Supabase organization and billing owner
- Primary database region
- Production domain and authentication callback URLs
- Support and abuse-report recipient
- Data retention and account-deletion policy

## Required before accepting transactions

- Whether WorkTrade is merely a communications marketplace or a party to agreements
- Prohibited and regulated work categories by launch jurisdiction
- Minimum participant age
- Dispute and cancellation rules
- Whether cash is settled independently or through WorkTrade
- Tax reporting and contractor-classification responsibilities

## Required before verification

- Which attributes are verified: email, phone, identity, licenses, insurance, or skill credentials
- Who may see verification evidence
- Verification provider, retention period, and appeal process

## Recommended initial boundary

Launch as a local matching and agreement-record service. Let participants settle cash and barter directly. Do not hold funds, create a platform currency, guarantee work, or claim to verify professional competence during the first beta. Require explicit consent for every obligation and clearly identify unverified claims.
